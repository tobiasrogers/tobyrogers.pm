Side A · A1

The Product Producer modelScaling strategic capability

The challenge
Our high-value Discover proposition only worked when a senior leader could be a genuine peer to the client at board level, and there were only three of us who could do it. Demand scaled with our calendars, not the market. The obvious fix (pushing Senior PMs into delivery work) would have solved utilisation and destroyed the strategic capability we'd built.
The move
I redefined the role from Product Owner to Product Producer: a senior product person who can talk strategy with a CEO about where to place the next bet and sequencing with a CPO about where value actually gets created, then make things real in the room with AI tooling. Domain experts bring board-level credibility; producers bring engagement ownership, product rigour and hands-on judgement. Neither works alone; together it's hard to replicate.
The outcome
80% of engagements now run on this model, and PM time has shifted from 60–80% tactical delivery to 70%+ strategic work. Strategic capability now scales with a developed bench, not a calendar.

A UK insurerNew proposition, validated

The challenge
A UK insurer wanted to capitalise on a gap in the market, but traditional delivery cycles made the opportunity too slow and too risky to commit to.
The move
I led the strategic framing of the proposition: what to validate, in what order, and what the commercial case had to prove at each gate before build investment was committed.
The outcome
A new proposition forecast (conservatively) to generate over £2.5m in premium revenue over three years.

B2

Under ArmourAI at the frontier

The challenge
Use AI to craft a genuinely motivational team talk for athletes, blending machine generation with human emotion so it felt authentic rather than synthetic.
The move
I led one of the agency's first AI-powered engagements: evaluating models, building the prompt framework and feedback loops that let the team iterate fast, and grounding the work in sports psychology so it resonated rather than impressed.
The outcome
An award-nominated campaign that set a benchmark for AI–human collaboration, and helped open the AI market that now accounts for over 60% of the agency's revenue.
